# F7: How student/hackathon teams in South Africa and Africa actually convert demos into first capital (Seedstars, angel syndicates, university funds, corporate innovation, NSFAS-adjacent grants, pitch competitions): real cheque sizes, timelines, and what separated winners from food-and-fun teams — including any CommuScore-adjacent Seedstars path.

## Findings

### [1] CommuScore converted a Seedstars Johannesburg pre-selection win (May 2017) into a slot at the SA final, where the global prize was up to US$500,000 equity investment in Switzerland.
- quote: "South African startups Invoiceworx, Empty Trips, CommuScore and Muzi have been named the winners of the Johannesburg pre-selection round of the South African leg of the Seedstars World competition... The overall winner will take part in the global final in Switzerland next year, giving it the opportunity to pitch for up to US$500,000 in equity investment."
- url: https://old.disruptafrica.com/2017/05/30/4-johannesburg-startups-advance-to-seedstars-sa-final/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2017-05-30
- confidence: high

### [2] The CommuScore-adjacent Seedstars capital ladder in SA (2017) was local pitch (Joburg/Cape Town satellites) → national final (29 June 2017, Johannesburg) → Swiss global final competing for up to US$500k equity, not a single cheque at demo day.
- quote: "It has now selected three wildcards to pitch alongside these startups at the final event on Thursday, June 29, the winner of which will proceed to the global finals in Switzerland to compete against other regional winners for up to US$500,000 in equity investment."
- url: https://old.disruptafrica.com/2017/06/28/seedstars-world-picks-3-wildcard-startups-for-sa-final/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2017-06-28
- confidence: high

### [3] CommuScore's actual first disclosed capital came from a corporate/BEE fintech track (Merrill Lynch South Africa + Royal Bafokeng Holdings via AlphaCode), not from winning Seedstars equity: ZAR1 million (≈US$70,000) each to four black-owned SA fintechs in late October/early November 2017.
- quote: "Four black-owned South African fintech startups have secured ZAR1 million (US$70,000) each in funding from Merrill Lynch at an event hosted by fintech club AlphaCode... The winning startups were alternative credit-scoring startup Commuscore, investment fraud insurance platform Investsure, insurance savings product Cascade, and SME finance advisory company Everest Ventures."
- url: https://old.disruptafrica.com/2017/11/06/sa-fintech-startups-secure-70k-each-from-merrill-lynch/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2017-11-06
- confidence: high

### [4] The same Merrill Lynch/AlphaCode prize also bundled non-cash capital (AlphaCode BEE Centre of Excellence membership) and was explicitly spent on tech platform build, skilled hires, equipment, and BD/marketing — the real post-demo playbook.
- quote: "each taking home the funding as well as membership of AlphaCode's BEE Centre of Excellence... We have seen that this funding is used for the development of technology platforms to create annuity revenue for the businesses; recruitment of highly skilled team members; purchasing of equipment such as laptops and boosting marketing and business development efforts"
- url: https://old.disruptafrica.com/2017/11/06/sa-fintech-startups-secure-70k-each-from-merrill-lynch/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2017-11-06
- confidence: high

### [5] Seedtable records CommuScore's only priced round as Seed US$59K led by Merrill Lynch on 1 Nov 2017 (founders Philile Mkhize and Priya Thakoor Mistry; founded 2017) and marks the company "No longer operating" — pitch wins did not equal survival.
- quote: "Seed · Merrill Lynch Lead · 59K USD · 1 Nov 2017... No longer operating... Funding Stage Seed... Total Funding 59K USD"
- url: https://seedtable.com/companies/commuscore
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-08-21 (profile last updated)
- confidence: medium

### [6] Seedstars World's own CommuScore profile still lists the venture at stage "dev" in Johannesburg, fintech, scoring informal savings groups (stokvels) — the pitch thesis that matched the Seedstars/Merrill informal-finance narrative.
- quote: "We provide an alternate credit score for the unbanked who are financially active in the informal sector to drive financial inclusion... Alternative credit scoring models based on payment and lending behaviours of informal savings groups in emerging markets."
- url: https://www.seedstarsworld.com/startup/42860/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live profile)
- confidence: high

### [7] A second conversion path is side-prizes and programme seats at Seedstars finals: at the 2019 Lausanne Summit, SA startup Iyeza won Gates Foundation US$10,000 (Vaccine Delivery Challenge), DRC's Schoolap won TRECC/School of Management Fribourg access to Seedstars Growth Programme "worth US$50,000", and Egypt's 7keema got Rising Tide Empowerment Prize (Growth Programme plus equity investment).
- quote: "South African startup Iyeza won the Seedstars Vaccine Delivery Challenge, awarded by the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation. The startup secured US$10,000... Schoolap... winning access to the Seedstars Growth Programme worth US$50,000... Egyptian startup 7keema... won the Rising Tide Foundation Empowerment Prize. Securing access to the Seedstars Growth Programme as well as an equity investment."
- url: https://old.disruptafrica.com/2019/04/08/seedstars-to-launch-100m-venture-fund-for-african-tech-startups/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2019-04-08
- confidence: high

### [8] Seedstars Africa Ventures was launched (April 2019, with First Growth Ventures) as a US$100m vehicle writing US$250,000–US$5 million early-stage cheques into Sub-Saharan startups — the post-competition equity path beyond the US$500k global prize.
- quote: "Seedstars has partnered the Paris-based VC firm First Growth Ventures to launch a US$100 million venture fund for Sub-Saharan African tech startups... early-stage investments of between US$250,000 and US$5 million"
- url: https://old.disruptafrica.com/2019/04/08/seedstars-to-launch-100m-venture-fund-for-african-tech-startups/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2019-04-08
- confidence: high

### [9] By Dec 2024 Seedstars Africa Ventures I had a US$42m first close (AfDB, EIB Global and others); EIB Global alone had announced a US$30m equity commitment in Jan 2024 — competition brand → institutional fund capital.
- quote: "Seedstars Africa Ventures I, a venture capital fund making early-stage investments in highly scalable start-ups in Africa, has achieved a first close of $42 million, with participation from the African Development Bank, EIB Global"
- url: https://www.seedstars.com/content-hub/newsroom/africa-investment-forum-2024-market-days-seedstars-africa-ventures-i-announces-42m-first-close/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-12-06
- confidence: high

### [10] Current Seedstars International Ventures published terms (2025 site): first ticket US$150–350k, follow-on up to US$500k, 3–5% ownership, average first check US$212k, 52% pre-seed, B2B focus — a concrete SA-relevant first-cheque band for post-demo raises.
- quote: "First Ticket $150-350k · Follow-on Ticket up to $500k · Ownership Target 3-5% · Customer Segment B2B · Average first check $212k · Allocation to Pre-Seed 52%"
- url: https://www.seedstars-international.vc/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025 (site snapshot)
- confidence: high

### [11] Side grants at Seedstars Africa Summit (Joburg 3–5 Dec 2019) were small but real: DOEN Land Restoration Prize US$10,000 grant plus nine months of Seedstars Investment Readiness Programme for finalists (from 220+ applications).
- quote: "the winner of which will take home US$10,000 in grant funding... More than 220 applications were received... All three startups have been awarded nine months of access to the Seedstars Investment Readiness Programme"
- url: https://old.disruptafrica.com/2019/11/26/3-startups-to-pitch-for-10k-doen-land-restoration-prize-at-seedstars-africa-summit/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2019-11-26
- confidence: high

### [12] SA SME Fund does not write founder cheques — it is a fund-of-funds (CEO Initiative / 50 largest JSE corporates) that backs VC managers; student/demo teams must target those managers (or the High Impact Seed Fund of Funds managers it backs) rather than apply to SA SME Fund directly.
- quote: "We do not make direct investments into SMEs. Instead, we empower specialist fund managers to do what they do best – identify, back, and grow exceptional entrepreneurs."
- url: https://sasmefund.co.za/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026 (live site)
- confidence: high

### [13] The university commercialisation capital stack for SA student/alumni teams is UTF (University Technology Fund): UTF I (2020, Seed–Series A; LPs SA SME Fund, SEFA, TIA, Stellenbosch, UCT), UTF II (2024, Series A/B including alumni-led), and UTF Seed Fund (2025, post-pilot/proof-of-concept spinouts and alumni startups) — supported under SA SME Fund's High Impact Seed Fund of Funds with DSI/TIA/E Squared/Allan & Gill Gray Philanthropies.
- quote: "Launched in 2020 with the support of the SA SME Fund, UTF is the first of its kind in Africa... UTF I focuses on commercialising university-originated IP through Seed Funding, Series Seed and Series A investments... The Seed Fund provides seed-stage capital to university spinouts and alumni-led startups post-pilot or proof of concept."
- url: https://utfund.co.za/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025
- confidence: high

### [14] What separates capital-ready teams from food-and-fun demos is written into SA VC mandates: Kalon Venture Partners will not consider applications that are not technology businesses and revenue-producing, and explicitly will not fund "capital for ideas or prototypes."
- quote: "Please note applications for investment which are not technology businesses and revenue producing will not be considered... Do you require growth capital and not capital for ideas or protoypes"
- url: https://www.kalonvp.com/entrepreneur/apply-for-funding/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live page)
- confidence: high

### [15] Post-DEMO-Africa visibility without capital: CommuScore pitched the Johannesburg DEMO Africa Innovation Tour leg (July 2018) but lost joint first to GetHosted and Loyal1; the Morocco main-event prize was "resources worth over US$150,000" (in-kind/resources, not a clean equity cheque).
- quote: "The other teams that participated were CommuScore, Afripav and You, Baby and I... will now pitch again... for the chance to take part in the main event in Morocco, where startups will be provided with resources worth over US$150,000."
- url: https://old.disruptafrica.com/2018/07/04/2-teams-winners-at-demo-africa-event-in-joburg/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2018-07-04
- confidence: high
