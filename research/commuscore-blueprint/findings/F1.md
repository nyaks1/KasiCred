# F1: CommuScore company profile: what product they actually sell, founders, HQ/city, target customers, traction, clients, and how their offering maps to informal-vendor credit scoring in South Africa.

## Findings

### [1] CommuScore (ZA) is an alternate credit-scoring provider that captures payment and lending behaviour of informal-sector consumers and micro-enterprises by giving informal saving schemes (stokvels) an administration/management tool that then builds credit profiles.
- quote: "We are an alternate credit scoring provider that captures payment and lending behaviors of consumers and micro-enterprises in the informal sector. We achieve this by providing informal saving schemes an administration and management tool. The use of the tool enables us to build a credit profile for consumers and micro-enterprises in the informal sector."
- url: https://www.linkedin.com/company/commuscore?originalSubdomain=za
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (LinkedIn company page; company founded 2017)
- confidence: high

### [2] CommuScore was founded in 2017 in Johannesburg by Philile Mkhize and Priya Mistry (Priya Thakoor Mistry).
- quote: "Commuscore — an alternate credit-scoring provider founded by Philile Mkhize and Priya Mistry."
- url: https://ventureburn.com/2017/11/merrill-lynch-royal-bafokeng-awards-r4m-four-black-fintech-startups/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2017-11 (Ventureburn article on the 31 Oct 2017 AlphaCode pitch; page re-dated on site)
- confidence: high

### [3] HQ is AlphaCode, 3rd Floor, 2 Merchant Place, Cnr Fredman Drive & Rivonia Road, Sandton/Johannesburg, Gauteng 2196 — the AlphaCode (RMI Holdings) fintech hub offices they received free as programme members.
- quote: "CommuScore / AlphaCode, 3rd Floor / 2 Merchant Place / Cnr Fredman Dr & Rivonia Rd / Sandton, 2196 / Gauteng, South Africa"
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20180203175018/https://commuscore.com/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2018-02-03 (Wayback capture of official site; © 2017 CommuScore)
- confidence: high

### [4] Target customers are stokvel members and informal micro-enterprises who are financially active but lack a formal credit identity — i.e. the unbanked/underbanked in SA informal savings groups.
- quote: "Low income earners financially active in stokvels are financially excluded from the formal financial market due to lack of credit identity."
- url: https://www.seedstarsworld.com/startup/42860/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (Seedstars World startup profile 42860)
- confidence: high

### [5] Product surface is a multi-tenant scheme-management web app: register a scheme, invite members, track meeting attendance, handle payments/loans/fines, plus dashboards and a Credit Request flow — the admin tool is the data-capture engine for scoring.
- quote: "Our solution helps to manage members of schemes, lending and all the great things that come with managing communities. ... Handle payments, loans and fines. ... Credit Request"
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20180203175018/https://commuscore.com/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2018-02-03
- confidence: high

### [6] On 31 October 2017 CommuScore won a R1-million (≈$59K–$70K) B-BBEE grant at AlphaCode’s pitching event run with Merrill Lynch South Africa and Royal Bafokeng Holdings; winners also joined AlphaCode’s BEE Centre of Excellence and got free Sandton office space.
- quote: "Four black-owned South African financial services startups each won R1-million last night at a pitching event hosted by AlphaCode. The event was held on 31 October and was run for the third time ... an initiative of Merrill Lynch South Africa and Royal Bafokeng Holdings"
- url: https://ventureburn.com/2017/11/four-black-owned-fintech-startups-win-r1m-alphacode-pitching-event/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2017-11-01 (event 2017-10-31)
- confidence: high

### [7] That R1m AlphaCode/Merrill Lynch award is the company’s only recorded capital: aggregators list a single Seed round of ~$59K–$70K (Nov 2017) with Merrill Lynch as the sole named investor — consistent with a grant mislabelled as equity seed.
- quote: "Seed ... Merrill Lynch Lead ... 59K USD ... 1 Nov 2017" (Seedtable) / "Total Funding $70K in 1 round ... Seed, Nov 06, 2017 ... Investors: Merrill Lynch" (Tracxn)

### [8] Competitions traction: CommuScore was one of 10 finalists in the Seedstars World South Africa final (June 2017, Standard Bank Auditorium, Rosebank, Johannesburg); EMGuidance won and represented SA — CommuScore did not win the national final.
- quote: "CommuScore – an alternative credit scoring model for credit identity that helps financially active people in stokvels who have no credit identity"
- url: https://ventureburn.com/2017/07/top-10-seedstars-south-africa-finalists/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2017-07
- confidence: high

### [9] Market thesis from their own pitch: informal savings schemes (stokvels) are already structured and disciplined; reputation risk drives contribution consistency — that social trust/discipline is the behavioural signal CommuScore digitizes into credit identity.
- quote: "Contrary to belief, informal saving schemes are highly structured and disciplined methods of savings that have existed in communities for hundreds of years. ... The risk of tarnishing an individual's societal reputation is what drives discipline for consistent contributions and attendance. Yet, trust and empathy are built into the mechanics of decision-making as a collective."
- url: https://www.linkedin.com/company/commuscore?originalSubdomain=za
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [10] Mapping to informal-vendor credit scoring for KasiCred: street vendors are typically stokvel/micro-enterprise participants without bureau files; CommuScore’s blueprint is B2B2C — sell free/cheap admin software to the group, extract contribution and loan-repayment behaviour as alternative data, then sell/issue credit identity to lenders. No named lender/bank clients or production volumes were found in any public source.
- quote: "Alternative credit scoring models based on payment and lending behaviours of informal savings groups in emerging markets."
- url: https://www.seedstarsworld.com/startup/42860/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: medium

### [11] CommuScore is defunct: Tracxn labels it “Deadpooled” with 1 employee as of Apr 2026; Seedtable marks it “No longer operating” / status “closed”; and the original domain commuscore.com has been repurposed as an SA sports-betting affiliate site (2026).
- quote: "CommuScore is a deadpooled company based in Sandton (South Africa), founded in 2017." (Tracxn) / "No longer operating" (Seedtable)
- url: https://www.tracxn.com/d/companies/commuscore/__fjW1paZSAKRZFb88hEgAbMM0fUFYpyvKttncMYfb6G4
- source_type: secondary
- published: Tracxn last updated 2026-07-30; Seedtable verified 2026-08-21
- confidence: high

### [12] DISAMBIGUATION: Comscore (comscore.com, one “m”) is Comscore, Inc. — a US cross-platform media/advertising measurement company — and is NOT CommuScore (two “m”, South African informal-sector alternative credit scoring, founded 2017, founders Philile Mkhize & Priya Mistry).
- quote: "Comscore is a trusted currency for planning, transacting, and evaluating media across platforms." vs CommuScore: "We are an alternate credit scoring provider that captures payment and lending behaviors of consumers and micro-enterprises in the informal sector."
- url: https://www.comscore.com/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026 (live site)
