## Competitive map (synthesis, not a single-source claim)

| Archetype | Example | Status | Why it lives / dies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stokvel-trust score SaaS | CommuScore | Dead (deadpooled; domain = betting SEO) | Score product without capital buyer; $70K; no distribution |
| MCA / working capital (formal SME) | Merchant Capital, Lula (ex-Lulalend) | Live / scaled | Underwrites banked SMEs with bank + card + bureau data; R40–50k/mo floor |
| Telco / mobile-money credit | M-Shwari, Fuliza (on M-PESA) | Live / scaled in Kenya | Owns the payment rail + transaction history; credit is a feature of the wallet |
| Mobile money in banked markets | M-PESA South Africa | Stalled / failed vs plan | ~70% banked + strict KYC = no unbanked beachhead |
| Alt-score engines sold to lenders | Tausi Africa | Operating (East Africa) | B2B LMS integration; alternative data; lender pays |
| Consumer score-repair + alt score | Fair Score (SA) | Pre-launch | No public traction yet |

**Who wins:** firms that either (a) already hold payment/card/mobile-money rails and can price credit as a product feature, or (b) lend working capital to SMEs that leave bank-statement / card / bureau traces, or (c) sell risk models B2B into licensed lenders’ LMS.

**Why others die:** pure “trust graph → score” products without a capital source or lender buyer; underwriting true cash-informal vendors with no digital exhaust; mobile-money clones in already-banked markets; tiny seed ($70K class) with no regulated partner.
