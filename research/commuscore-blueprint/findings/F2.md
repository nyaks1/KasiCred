# F2: How CommuScore raised approximately $70k (or any documented capital): every funding source, prize, grant, accelerator, angel, or Seedstars outcome, with dates, amounts, and the milestones that unlocked each cheque.

## Findings

### [1] CommuScore's entire documented capital is a single ZAR1,000,000 (~US$70,000) grant won on 31 October 2017 at AlphaCode's B-BBEE fintech pitch, funded by Merrill Lynch South Africa and Royal Bafokeng Holdings — not VC equity and not Seedstars.
- quote: "Four black-owned South African fintech startups have secured ZAR1 million (US$70,000) each in funding from Merrill Lynch at an event hosted by fintech club AlphaCode."
- url: https://old.disruptafrica.com/2017/11/06/sa-fintech-startups-secure-70k-each-from-merrill-lynch/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2017-11-06
- confidence: high

### [2] The cheque was grant funding (programme range R500,000–R1,000,000 per black-owned fintech), not a priced equity round; AlphaCode/Merrill/Royal Bafokeng had disbursed R13m to 15 startups over the prior two years by this event.
- quote: "To date, the programme has disbursed R13-million in grant funding to 15 businesses over the past two years (ranging from R500 000 to R1-million per business)."
- url: https://ventureburn.com/2017/11/merrill-lynch-royal-bafokeng-awards-r4m-four-black-fintech-startups/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2017-11-05 (article date later restamped; event week of 2017-10-31)
- confidence: high

### [3] Milestone that unlocked the R1m: winning one of four grants after a five-minute pitch in front of a ten-company field of black-owned SA financial-services startups, judged on revenue model, problem, differentiation, and marketing plans.
- quote: "Monetary awards were made to four of the 10 black-owned financial services businesses which pitched. … The winning businesses were selected because of their ability to meet our judging criteria which included their revenue model, the problem they are solving, how they differentiate from their competitors and their marketing plans."
- url: https://ventureburn.com/2017/11/four-black-owned-fintech-startups-win-r1m-alphacode-pitching-event/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2017-11-01 (event 2017-10-31)
- confidence: high

### [4] Co-winners of the same R4m (R1m each) cheque were Investsure, Cascade, and Everest Ventures; CommuScore's co-founder Priya Mistry is named in the winner photo caption alongside Merrill Lynch SA and Royal Bafokeng Holdings executives.
- quote: "Co-founder CommuScore Priya Mistry, Merrill Lynch’s regional executive of operations and corporate affairs head Julia Benadie, Royal Bafokeng Holdings CEO Albertina Kekana."
- url: https://ventureburn.com/2017/11/merrill-lynch-royal-bafokeng-awards-r4m-four-black-fintech-startups/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2017-11-05
- confidence: high

### [5] Non-cash capital unlocked with the grant: membership in AlphaCode's BEE Centre of Excellence plus free office space at AlphaCode, 2 Merchant Place, Sandton — the address CommuScore still listed as HQ on its own site into Dec 2018.
- quote: "The four businesses, as recipients of the grant funding, will become members of AlphaCode’s BEE Centre of Excellence and will receive additional business support services including free office space at a prime Sandton location."
- url: https://ventureburn.com/2017/11/four-black-owned-fintech-startups-win-r1m-alphacode-pitching-event/

- published: 2017-11-01

- quote: "After 10 startups pitched to a panel of judges, four startups from Gauteng were chosen to advance to the grand final. They are inventory financing platform Invoiceworx, smart transport marketplace Empty Trips, savings and loans startup CommuScore, and e-health startup Muzi."

- quote: "with Cape Town startup EMGuidance clinching top spot.
