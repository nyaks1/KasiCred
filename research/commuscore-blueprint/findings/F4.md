# F4: South Africa credit bureau market and regulation relevant to informal-sector scoring: bureau products and real pricing, the formal credit data gap for cash-only vendors, NCR registration, National Credit Act, POPIA/section 71 automated decision rules, and whether community/trust scores are "credit information."

## Findings

### [1] The National Credit Regulator (NCR) was established by the National Credit Act 34 of 2005 (NCA) and registers credit bureaux, credit providers, debt counsellors, ADR agents and payment distribution agents.
- quote: "The National Credit Regulator (NCR) was established as the regulator for the South African credit industry by the National Credit Act (34 of 2005) (NCA). ... The NCR registers and ensures compliance to the NCA by the following industry participants: credit providers, credit bureaus, debt counsellors, alternative dispute resolution agents and payment distribution agents."
- url: https://www.ncr.org.za/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (site accessed 2026-09-24)
- confidence: high

### [2] The NCA expressly requires the NCR to promote an accessible credit market for historically disadvantaged persons, low income persons, and remote/low-density communities — the statutory hook for informal-sector inclusion.
- quote: "The NCA requires the NCR to promote the development of an accessible credit market, particularly to address the needs of historically disadvantaged persons, low income persons, and remote, isolated or low density communities."
- url: https://www.ncr.org.za/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (site accessed 2026-09-24)
- confidence: high

### [3] NCA long title lists as core purposes: regulate credit information; register credit bureaux, credit providers and debt counselling services; and establish the NCR and National Consumer Tribunal.
- quote: "to provide for debt re-organisation in cases of over-indebtedness; to regulate credit information; to provide for registration of credit bureaux, credit providers and debt counselling services; ... to establish the National Credit Regulator and the National Consumer Tribunal"
- url: https://www.gov.za/documents/national-credit-act
- source_type: primary
- published: National Credit Act 34 of 2005 (assent/commencement: NCA came into effect June 2006)
- confidence: high

### [4] NCA credit-information provisions are in Chapter 2 Part B; sections 67, 68, 70 and 72 commenced 1 September 2006, and section 71 commenced 1 June 2007 (NCA s71 is not the same as POPIA s71).
- quote: "1 September 2006, Chapter 2 part B, Sections 67, 68, 70 and 72 (Gazette 28824 of 11 May 2006) ... 1 June 2007, Chapter 4 part A, Section 71, Chapter 4 parts C and D, Chapter 5, Chapter 6 and Section 163"
- url: https://www.gov.za/documents/national-credit-act
- source_type: primary
- published: NCA 34 of 2005 commencement schedule (Gazette 28824 of 11 May 2006)
- confidence: high

### [5] As of the NCR homepage counters, 53 credit bureaus and 8,218 credit providers are NCR-registered; the public credit-bureau register page reports 55 registered records including TransUnion (NCRCB4), XDS (NCRCB5), Consumer Profile Bureau (NCRCB2) and CrossCheck (NCRCB1), most with Final Registration Date 2006/09/01.
- quote: "53 Credit Bureaus ... 8218 Credit Providers" and "Records found: 55 ... TransUnion Credit Bureau (Pty) Ltd ... NCR Registration No. NCRCB4 ... Final Registration Date: 2006/09/01"
- url: https://www.ncr.org.za/ and http://www.ncr.org.za/register_of_registrants/registered_cb1.php
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (site accessed 2026-09-24)
- confidence: high

### [6] Operating as a credit bureau (or reseller credit bureau) requires NCR registration; the Registrations Department is mandated to register both credit bureaus and reseller credit bureaus and renew their registrations.
- quote: "The Registrations Department is mandated to register credit providers, credit bureaus, debt counsellors, payment distribution agents, alternative dispute resolution agents and reseller credit bureaus."
- url: https://www.ncr.org.za/index.php/departments/registrations-compliance
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (site accessed 2026-09-24)
- confidence: high

### [7] NCR Credit Bureau Compliance regulates bureau operations under the NCA and Regulations and enforces credit reporting standards, maximum retention periods for paid-up judgments/adverse information, and confidentiality of consumer credit information.
- quote: "Credit Information Department , regulates  the operations of credit bureaus to ensure that they comply with the provisions of the National Credit Act, 2005 and the Regulations thereto (“NCA”). ... The Department ensures that all credit bureaus adhere to maximum retention period of consumer credit information such as paid up judgments and adverse information and protection and confidentiality of the information."
- url: https://www.ncr.org.za/index.php/departments/compliance-credit-bureau
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (site accessed 2026-09-24)
- confidence: high

### [8] POPIA section 71 bars decisions with legal or substantial effect that are based solely on automated processing intended to provide a profile of a person — expressly including credit worthiness, reliability and conduct — unless a contract exception applies or appropriate measures protect the data subject's legitimate interests.
- quote: "a data subject may not be subject to a decision which results in legal consequences for him, her or it, or which affects him, her or it to a substantial degree, which is based solely on the basis of the automated processing of personal information intended to provide a profile of such person including his or her performance at work, or his, her or its credit worthiness, reliability, location, health, personal preferences or conduct."
- url: https://popia.co.za/section-71-automated-decision-making/
- source_type: secondary (enacted POPIA s71 text reformatted as a website by Accessible Law/Michalsons; text is the English signed by the President)
- published: posted 20 September 2019 (POPIA 4 of 2013 text)
- confidence: high

### [9] POPIA s71(2)–(3) exceptions require, when relying on contract/legitimate-interest measures, that the data subject may make representations and receive sufficient information about the underlying logic of the automated processing — i.e. scoring products need an explanation/contest path, not a black-box deny.
- quote: "The appropriate measures ... must— (a) provide an opportunity for a data subject to make representations about a decision referred to in subsection (1); and (b) require a responsible party to provide a data subject with sufficient information about the underlying logic of the automated processing of the information relating to him or her to enable him or her to make representations"
- url: https://popia.co.za/section-71-automated-decision-making/
- source_type: secondary (enacted POPIA s71 text on reformatted official-text site)
- published: posted 20 September 2019 (POPIA 4 of 2013 text)
- confidence: high

### [10] Community/trust scores used to judge credit worthiness or reliability fall inside POPIA s71's automated-profiling scope even if not held by a registered bureau: s71 names "credit worthiness, reliability, ... personal preferences or conduct" as profile categories; whether they are also NCA "credit information" (triggering bureau registration/retention rules) turns on the NCA s1 definition and who holds/uses them, which could not be extracted from the NCA PDF in this run.
- quote: "his, her or its credit worthiness, reliability, location, health, personal preferences or conduct"
- url: https://popia.co.za/section-71-automated-decision-making/
- source_type: secondary (enacted POPIA s71 text; NCA s1 definition not textually verified here)
- published: posted 20 September 2019
- confidence: medium

### [11] POPIA as a whole commenced 1 July 2020 (one-year grace to 30 June 2021); Information Officers must be registered with the Information Regulator before acting under s55 — operational compliance cost for any scoring platform.
- quote: "It was 1 July 2020, and the one-year grace period to comply ended on 30 June 2021." and "Information Officers must assume their duties only after the responsible party has registered them with the Regulator."
- url: https://popia.co.za/ and https://www.inforegulator.org.za/
- source_type: primary (Information Regulator) + secondary (popia.co.za commencement summary matching gov.za POPIA page)
- published: gov.za POPIA commencement notices (Gazette 43461 of 22 June 2020; Gazette 44383 of 1 April 2021)
- confidence: high

### [12] Formal SA bureau data products are built from formal-sector registers: XDS's "comprehensive XDS Credit Report incorporates the complete National Loans Register (NLR), South African Credit & Risk Reporting Association (SACRRA) data sets and detailed property information and business and director data from the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC)" — cash-only vendor transactions are not among those sources.
- quote: "Our comprehensive XDS Credit Report incorporates the complete National Loans Register (NLR), South African Credit & Risk Reporting Association (SACRRA) data sets and detailed property information and business and director data from the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC)."
- url: https://www.xds.co.za/what-we-do/
- source_type: primary (bureau vendor product description)
- published: unknown (site accessed 2026-09-24; footer © 2023)
- confidence: high

### [13] The NCR has published a primary research report titled "Alternative Data Landscape in South Africa" (June 2021) — the regulator's own work product on data outside traditional bureau files; full PDF body text was not machine-extracted in this run (PDF returned as binary).
- quote: "June 2021 ... Alternative Data Landscape in South Africa"
- url: https://www.ncr.org.za/index.php/publications/research-reports and http://www.ncr.org.za/documents/Alternative%20Data%20Landscape%20in%20South%20Africa%2024.6.21.pdf
- source_type: primary
- published: June 2021
- confidence: high (existence/date) / low (interior claims not extracted)

### [14] NCR's research library documents formal credit-coverage gaps at the informal/SME margin: "Literature Review on SME Access to Credit in South Africa" (Dec 2011), "Information Sharing and SMME Financing in South Africa" (PERC, 2008), "Access and Use of credit by poor households in Johannesburg" (2008), and "Credit and Borrowing in South Africa, FinScope Consumer Survey South Africa 2012" (2013).
- quote: "Literature Review on SME Access to Credit in South Africa Final Report ... Information Sharing and SMME Financing in South Africa ... Access and Use of credit by poor households in Johannesburg ... Credit and Borrowing in South Africa, FinScope Consumer Survey South Africa 2012"
- url: https://www.ncr.org.za/index.php/publications/research-reports
- source_type: primary
- published: 2008–2013 (titles/dates as listed)
- confidence: high (catalogue entries) / low (interior findings not extracted)

### [15] Bureau product families on the market (from a registered bureau's site) are customer/lifecycle solutions plus generic and custom analytics products, not published rate cards; XDS markets free consumer reports via Splendi while business APIs/pricing are quote-based — so "real pricing" for bureau pulls is not published on primary bureau sites and must be obtained by RFP.
- quote: "XDS solutions cover the entire business cycle of the company and credit life cycle of the customer" / "Get your credit report and score for free on Splendi and access it as many times as you like." / "GENERIC ANALYTICS SOLUTIONS / CUSTOM ANALYTICS PRODUCTS"
- url: https://www.xds.co.za/ and https://www.xds.co.za/what-we-do/
- source_type: primary (bureau vendor)
- published: unknown (site accessed 2026-09-24)
- confidence: medium
