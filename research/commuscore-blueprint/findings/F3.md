# F3: CommuScore business model and unit economics: who pays (lender vs vendor vs consumer), pricing/packaging, and how that compares to SA credit bureau list prices — TransUnion Trace R20.84 excl VAT, Consumer Profile R55.58, Business Dynamic Rating/SME Assessment R457.01, consumer subscription R21/month or R145/year (validate or correct these).

## Findings

### [1] CommuScore (founded 2017, Sandton/Johannesburg) is a two-sided model: a free mobile admin/management tool for informal savings groups (stokvels/eSusus/Chamas) that captures member payment and lending behaviour, which is then used to build an alternative credit score for consumers and micro-enterprises who are unbanked or "underscored."
- quote: "Our solution enables community based saving scheme members (e.g. informal savings groups, Stokvels, eSusus, Chamas) to manage their groups payment history, meetings and loans by members and other functionalities that enables us to capture and track payment behaviours of individuals. We are able to create a credit score using these behaviours."
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20180817174130/http://commuscore.com/about/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2018-08-17 (archive capture of CommuScore About Us)
- confidence: high

### [2] CommuScore's product packaging is three features on the group-management app — Manage your group (members + lending/payment behaviours), Schedule Meetings, and Credit identity ("Your lending and payment behaviours can create a credit identity for you") — with no published price, tier, or fee for any of them on the live site (2018–2019 captures).
- quote: "Manage your members and their lending and payment behaviours using your mobile … Credit identity — Your lending and payment behaviours can create a credit identity for you"
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20190405084136/http://commuscore.com/services/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2019-04-05 (archive capture of CommuScore Services)
- confidence: high

### [3] CommuScore never published who pays (lender vs vendor vs consumer) or any unit price; the only revenue-adjacent signals on the primary site are "PARTNERING BANKS: FIRST RAND BANK, STANDARD BANK" and funder/award credits (SAB Foundation Social Innovation Awards 3rd place 2017; Merrill Lynch Bank of America winner 2017), which imply a B2B2C data/score sale to banks rather than vendor or consumer subscriptions — but this is inference, not a stated model.
- quote: "FUNDERS — SAB FOUNDATION- SOCIAL INNOVATION AWARDS WINNER (3RD PLACE, 2017); MERRILL LYNCH BANK OF AMERICA- WINNER (2017). PARTNERING BANKS — FIRST RAND BANK; STANDARD BANK."
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20180817174130/http://commuscore.com/about/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2018-08-17
- confidence: medium

- quote: "CommuScore is a deadpooled company based in Sandton (South Africa), founded in 2017. … CommuScore has raised $70K in funding. … Stage: Deadpooled … Latest Funding Round: Seed, Nov 06, 2017 … Employee Count: 1 as on Apr 30, 2026"

### [10] TransUnion SA Business Credit Report (public product, 2020) is sold as a self-check "know the credit status of your business" report via mytransunion.co.za/Products/MyBusiness/YBU001 with no list price shown; content = registration details, civil court records, defaults, banking details, property/bond, 3-year enquiry history — closest public analogue to the seed's "Business Dynamic Rating/SME Assessment," but product name and price are unconfirmed.
- quote: "Make better and informed business decisions. Get Your Business Report Now! … Registration detail of the company including civil court records and default information. Banking details on the company. Default data relating to non-payment of accounts. Property and Bond information. Enquiry history on the company within the last three years."
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20200803091351/https://www.transunion.co.za/product/business-credit-report
- source_type: primary
- published: 2020-08-03
- confidence: high (product exists; no price on page)

### [11] SA bureau consumer-direct pricing floor is collapsing toward free: Experian SA offers unlimited free consumer credit report + score (0–999) on Up (up.experian.co.za), and XDS offers free report/score on Splendi, while TransUnion SA still sold paid TrueCredit/TrueIdentity subscriptions as of 2020 (and one free report/year is NCA-mandated). Implication for KasiCred unit economics: charging vendors/consumers for a score is competing with free; the viable payer is the lender/credit provider buying enquiry-time data.
- quote: "Experian provides free credit reports and free credit scores on Up, Experian's progressive web-app. … Your credit score is calculated via a Credit Bureau check … Experian evaluates all your accounts … to assign you a credit score of 0–999."
- url: https://www.experian.co.za/consumer/my-free-credit-check-and-your-free-credit-report
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live page fetched 2026-09; © 2026 Experian)
- confidence: high

### [12] Competitor B2B packaging (XDS, Mettus) is lifecycle-based with many SKUs and no public prices: Account Origination (IDV, Affordability, Presage Score, Consumer Credit Application, Business Standard/Detailed Online Enquiry, CIPC, Commercial Trace, etc.), Account Management, Collections, Tracing (Trace, Trace Plus, Commercial Trace), XBRL. This is the realistic competitive price-list shape for KasiCred's B2B side — per-enquiry SKUs sold to credit providers, not consumer subscriptions.
- quote: "CONSUMER PRODUCTS: Trace, Trace Plus … COMMERCIAL PRODUCTS: CIPC, CIPC Plus, Business Standard Online Enquiry, Business Detailed Online Enquiry, Commercial Trace, Adverse Report …"
- url: https://www.xds.co.za/customer-solutions/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live XDS site; footer © 2023)
- confidence: high
