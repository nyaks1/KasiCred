# Research brief — CommuScore blueprint for KasiCred

> Date: 2026-09-24 · depth: **deep** · workspace: `research/commuscore-blueprint/`

## Refined question

How did **CommuScore** (South African alternative-credit / community-scoring startup) build and fund a business closest to **KasiCred** (blockchain trust + credit ledger for informal street vendors) — specifically their product, ~**$70k** raise path, business model and unit economics against real SA credit-bureau pricing — and what peer-reviewed evidence proves the informal-sector credit-invisibility problem KasiCred targets?

Audience: Nyakallo (WeThinkCode_ student, KasiCred co-founder). Decision at stake: whether to treat CommuScore as a fundable blueprint, copy/steal their model, or differentiate hard — and how to fund a hackathon-born SA fintech without fantasy money.

## Scope

**In**
- CommuScore: founders, product, market, traction, funding rounds/sources (~$70k), pitch narrative, business model, pricing, customers (who pays)
- User-supplied dossier URLs (LinkedIn ZA, Seedstars, Tracxn, Crunchbase, Seedtable) + disambiguation of `comscore.com` (Comscore media ≠ CommuScore)
- SA credit bureau market economics: TransUnion / Experian / local bureaus; informal-sector gaps; NCR / Credit Act / POPIA constraints
- Academic + institutional evidence that informal / cash-only vendors lack credit trails and that alternative data works (SA, Africa, Global South)
- Comparable alt-credit / financial-inclusion startups (winners and corpses)
- Hackathon → startup → grant/seed playbook relevant to SA / African fintech (Seedstars, angels, grants)

**Out**
- Building features for KasiCred
- Generic “startup advice” with no SA/fintech numbers
- US credit-scoring deep dives (FICO) except as contrast
- Full blockchain protocol design

## Assumptions (not re-asked)

1. “CommuScore” is a ZA community/alternative credit-scoring startup; `comscore.com` is a different company (media measurement) and must not be mixed in.
2. The ~$70k figure is approximate and may be total non-dilutive + prize + angel; verify, do not round up.
3. User-supplied TransUnitrans/TransUnion SA list prices (below) are treated as market anchors to validate or correct, not gospel.
4. KasiCred context: QR/WhatsApp ratings → Celo hashed trust score → Loan Ready badge; team Nyakallo · Khatisani · Liya.
5. Time frame for “recent” growth/funding news: last 36 months, prefer 2023–2026.
