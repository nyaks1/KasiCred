KasiCred Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · Contact: nyakallo@example.com

Who we are

KasiCred helps informal traders create a proof-of-income pack they can share with a lender or landlord they choose. We do not provide credit and we do not issue credit scores.

What we collect

  • WhatsApp phone number (to run the conversation)
  • Stall / trading name and market area
  • Trade amounts and confirmed-sale amounts
  • Optional photos (till slips, stokvel pages) if the trader sends them
  • Consent record (that the trader agreed to processing)

We do not collect GPS location, race, health, or biometrics.

Why we process it (POPIA)

We process personal information with the trader’s consent to produce an income-evidence pack the trader may share. Processing is limited to that purpose (minimality). Traders are told this before we collect anything.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. A pack is shared only when the trader gives a share code / link to a chosen party (for example a microlender). Every fetch can be logged for the trader.

Retention & deletion

We keep data only as long as needed for the pack and any legal duty. Traders can ask us to delete their data by messaging “DELETE” on WhatsApp or emailing the contact above.

WhatsApp

Messages are handled via the WhatsApp Business Platform. Message content used for the pack is stored only for the purposes above. Do not send passwords or bank PINs.

Children

This service is for adult traders and adult buyers.

Changes

We may update this policy for the hackathon prototype and beyond. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
